# Kenney assets

The furniture and character GLB models in this folder come from Kenney's
Furniture Kit 2.0 and Blocky Characters 2.0. Both packs are licensed CC0.

- Furniture Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- Blocky Characters: https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters

The active game uses the furniture kit's table, cushioned chair, and POS
computer models. The character models and their texture atlases are included
for future customer and staff variations.
